# Tillot

**Your phone, on your plan.**

Tell Tillot what you want to get done. It turns it into one focused task, with a time limit and only the apps and websites that task needs. Everything else stays out of reach until you're done.

- **An AI planner, not a to-do list.** Say "study chapter 4 for an hour" and the task is set, apps and all.
- **Holds you to your own rules.** It remembers your goals, deadlines and routines, and pushes back when you drift.
- **Blocks the distractions.** Apps and sites outside your task are unavailable, with a few emergency passes when you really need one.
- **Counts real progress.** Time in the apps that matter, or a countdown for work away from the screen.
- **Synced and private.** Your tasks follow you between devices, encrypted on your device before they're stored.
